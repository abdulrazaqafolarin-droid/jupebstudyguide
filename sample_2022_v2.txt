Q20:
  Q: Differentiate
$$e^{x\cos x}$$
  Expl (first 500): Let
$$y=e^{x\cos x}.$$
Use the chain rule:
$$\frac{dy}{dx}
=e^{x\cos x}\cdot\frac{d}{dx}(x\cos x).$$
Differentiate $x\cos x$ using the product rule:
$$\frac{d}{dx}(x\cos x)=\cos x-x\sin x.$$
Therefore,
$$\boxed{\frac{dy}{dx}=e^{x\cos x}(\cos x-x\sin x)}.$$
  Options:
    A: $e^{x\cos x}(\cos x-x\sin x)$
    B: $e^{x(\cos x-x\sin x)}$
    C: $e^{x\cos x}\sin x$
    D: $e^{x\cos x}\cos x$
