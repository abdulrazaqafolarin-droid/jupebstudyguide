A weak acid has a ______ conjugate base and a strong base has a ______ conjugate acid.
   A. weak, weak
   B. strong, strong
   C. weak, strong
   D. strong, weak
   Correct Answer: D
   Explanation: According to the Brønsted-Lowry theory, a weak acid produces a relatively strong conjugate base, while a strong base produces a relatively weak conjugate acid. However, the relationship is inverse: a weak acid has a strong conjugate base, and a strong base has a weak conjugate acid. Wait, let's re-evaluate the logic: A weak acid (doesn't fully dissociate) has a conjugate base that is strong enough to pull protons back. Actually, the rule is: the weaker the acid, the stronger its conjugate base. Therefore, a weak acid has a strong conjugate base, and a strong base has a weak conjugate acid. The stated answer D (strong, weak) is correct.
