--- CHEMISTRY 2015 OBJECTIVE Q1 ---
A normal body temperature is $37^{\circ}\text{C}$, $K_{\text{w}} = 2.4 \times 10^{-14}$. Calculate $[\text{H}_3\text{O}^+]$ and $[\text{OH}^-]$ for the body fluid at this temperature
   A. $[\text{H}_3\text{O}^+] = [\text{OH}^-] = 2.5 \times 10^{-7}$
   B. $[\text{H}_3\text{O}^+] = [\text{H}^-] = 1.5 \times 10^{-8}$
