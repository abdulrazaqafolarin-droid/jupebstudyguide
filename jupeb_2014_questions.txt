--- PHYSICS 2014 OBJECTIVE Q1 ---
A concave spherical mirror has a radius of curvature $20\text{ cm}$. What is the nature of the image of an object placed $10.0\text{ cm}$ in front of it?
   A. $-10.0\text{ cm}$, real, inverted, diminished
   B. no image is formed, possibly at infinity
   C. $20.0\text{ cm}$ real, upright and magnified
   D. $-10.0\text{ cm}$, virtual, upright and magnified
   Correct Answer: B
   Explanation: The focal length $f$ is $R/2 = 10\text{ cm}$. Since the object is placed at the focal point, the reflected rays are parallel, and the image is formed at infinity.

--- PHYSICS 2014 OBJECTIVE Q3 ---
What is the critical angle for an air water boundary with refractive indices of air and water being $1.00$ and $1.33$ respectively?
   A. $42.0$
   B. $30.0$
   C. $48.8$
   D. $50.0$
   Correct Answer: C
   Explanation: The critical angle $\theta_{c}$ is calculated using the formula $\sin(\theta_{c}) = \frac{n_{1}}{n_{2}}$, where $n_{1}$ is the refractive index of the denser medium ($1.33$) and $n_{2}$ is the refractive index of the rarer medium ($1.00$).

For a medium transmitting a longitudinal wave, the areas of the medium where the density is called ___________?
   A. rarefaction
   B. compressions
   C. density holes
   D. pressure holes
   Correct Answer: B
   Explanation: In a longitudinal wave, compressions are the regions where the particles of the medium are crowded together, resulting in high density and pressure.
